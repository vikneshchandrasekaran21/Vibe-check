# Lawsuit magnets and jurisdictions

Load this file for every Full and Enterprise check of an app with real users. It covers the **specific, easy-to-detect website and app basics that people actually get sued, fined or sent demand letters over**. Many are hunted by automated crawlers, so small apps are targets too.

**Rules:** you are not a lawyer. Detect the trap in the code or on the live site, cite the evidence, and put the legal judgement on the "Needs a lawyer" list. What applies depends on **where the users are**, not where the business is. Use the regions from Step 1, and ask if they're unknown. Never tell the user they're "compliant".

## Detect in the code (every app)

- [ ] 🔴 LAW-001 **Third-party fonts loaded from outside servers:** Google Fonts or other font CDNs loaded from `fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`, `use.typekit.net` or similar send every visitor's IP address to that company. In Germany and Austria this triggered a wave of automated demand letters (€100–€500 each) after a 2022 Munich court ruling. **Fix:** self-host the font files (`next/font` self-hosts Google Fonts by default; otherwise download them into `public/`). Grep CSS, HTML, `<link>` tags and `@import`.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-002 **Trackers fire before consent:** analytics, ad pixels (Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, LinkedIn), session replay (Hotjar, Clarity, FullStory, LogRocket), heatmaps and live chat or chatbot widgets must not load until the user agrees (where consent is required). In California, thousands of wiretap (CIPA) lawsuits target exactly these tools, with statutory damages claimed per visitor. Check script tags, tag managers and SDK init calls for consent gating.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-003 **Third-party embeds load before consent:** YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, social media embeds, reCAPTCHA and similar also send visitor data. Use click-to-load placeholders or privacy-enhanced modes (e.g. `youtube-nocookie.com`), and disclose them.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-004 **Chat and session replay capture sensitive input:** chat widgets, session replay and form analytics must not record passwords, payment, health or children's data. Inputs are masked, and the tool is disclosed in the privacy policy.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-005 **Accessibility basics on revenue paths:** sign-up, login, product pages, cart, checkout and booking work with keyboard only and a screen reader (labels, alt text, focus, contrast). Thousands of US ADA website lawsuits are filed every year, most against small online businesses. **Accessibility overlay widgets don't prevent lawsuits** (many suits target sites using them). Flag any overlay used *instead of* real fixes.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-006 **Subscriptions and auto-renewal:** before charging, clearly show price, billing frequency, renewal terms and how to cancel, next to the "subscribe" button. Get **separate, affirmative consent** (no pre-ticked box), keep proof of consent, send renewal reminders, and offer **online cancellation that's as easy as sign-up** (a visible "cancel" link, with no forced phone calls or endless retention screens). This is required in California for its residents, and similar rules exist elsewhere.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-007 **Marketing email and SMS have proof of opt-in:** consent is separate from the terms, unticked by default, recorded (who, when, how, what wording), and every message has a working one-click unsubscribe. Canada (CASL) requires express opt-in for most commercial messages, with large fines. US texts are covered by TCPA rules.
- [ ] 🔴 LAW-008 **No fake urgency, fake reviews or hidden fees:** countdown timers that reset, "only 2 left" that isn't true, made-up testimonials, drip pricing (fees added at checkout) and pre-ticked add-ons attract regulator action in the US, EU, UK, India and elsewhere.
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-009 **Cookie banner is fair:** "Reject all" is as easy as "Accept all" (same layer, same prominence) for EU and UK users, choices are remembered, and consent can be withdrawn later. Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals are honoured for California users.
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-010 **Legal notice / Impressum** if the app targets users in Germany, Austria or Switzerland, or makes money there (even through ads or affiliate links): the legal name, address, contact email and phone, company register and VAT ID where applicable, reachable from every page in **two clicks or fewer** (usually the footer). Missing or hidden notices lead to warning letters and fines.
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-011 **French for Quebec users:** if the app sells to or serves people in Quebec, the website, consent banner, privacy notice and commercial content must be available in French (Bill 96, Law 25).
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-012 **Biometric data** (face scans, face filters, voice prints, fingerprints): explicit written consent, a published retention and destruction policy, and no selling. Illinois (BIPA) allows private lawsuits per person, and other states and the EU treat it as sensitive data.
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-013 **Video viewing data shared with ad pixels:** if the app shows videos and sends what a user watched to Meta or other ad platforms, US Video Privacy Protection Act lawsuits are a known risk. Don't send video titles or URLs to pixels without consent.
- [ ] 🟡 LAW-014 **Children:** apps directed at children, or that know they have child users, follow COPPA (US, under 13), GDPR-K, the UK Children's Code and India DPDP (under 18). See `kids-education.md`.

## Country and region table

Use the regions from Step 1. Each row lists the traps to check for users in that place. Mark each row that applies in the report, and send judgement calls to the lawyer list.

| Users in | Main laws to name | Traps to check |
|---|---|---|
| **European Union** | GDPR, ePrivacy (cookies), Digital Services Act (platforms), European Accessibility Act (many consumer services since June 2025), consumer rights rules | Self-hosted fonts and consent before trackers; fair cookie banner; privacy policy with legal basis and transfers outside the EU; 14-day withdrawal right and clear pricing for online sales; accessibility for e-commerce and banking-type services; notice-and-action and transparency duties for platforms with user content |
| **Germany, Austria, Switzerland** | GDPR, DDG §5 (Impressum), UWG (competition warning letters), Swiss FADP | Impressum within two clicks; Google Fonts and other IP leaks; correct cookie consent; complete and accurate privacy policy (competitors can send warning letters) |
| **United Kingdom** | UK GDPR, PECR (cookies, marketing), Online Safety Act (user-to-user and search services), Children's Code | Cookie consent with fair reject; marketing opt-in; risk assessments and reporting tools if users can share content or talk to each other; age-appropriate design for child users |
| **United States: California** | CCPA/CPRA, CIPA (wiretap), Automatic Renewal Law, CalOPPA | "Do Not Sell or Share" link and GPC support where data is shared for ads; no pixels, replay or chat before consent; auto-renewal consent, reminders and click-to-cancel; privacy policy with a California section |
| **United States: all** | ADA (accessibility), COPPA, TCPA (texts and calls), CAN-SPAM, ROSCA (online subscriptions), FTC Act (deceptive practices), state privacy laws (Virginia, Colorado, Texas and others), Illinois BIPA, VPPA | Accessibility on revenue paths; children under 13; SMS consent; email unsubscribe and address; subscription disclosures; honest marketing and reviews; biometric consent; video data in pixels |
| **Canada** | PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, Quebec Bill 96 (French), CASL, Ontario AODA (accessibility) | Opt-in before tracking for Quebec users; French versions for Quebec; express consent for marketing messages; privacy officer contact; accessibility for larger Ontario organisations |
| **India** | DPDP Act and Rules, IT Act and IT Rules (intermediaries), Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, CERT-In directions, RBI rules for payments | Notice and consent in clear language; verifiable parental consent for under-18s; grievance officer for platforms; seller and price disclosures for e-commerce; incident reporting within 6 hours to CERT-In; card and recurring-payment rules |
| **Brazil** | LGPD, Consumer Defense Code | Legal basis and data protection officer contact; consumer rights for online sales, including a 7-day withdrawal |
| **Australia** | Privacy Act, Spam Act, Australian Consumer Law | Privacy policy, consented marketing with unsubscribe, honest pricing and no misleading claims |
| **Japan, South Korea, Singapore and others** | APPI (Japan), PIPA (Korea), PDPA (Singapore) | Purpose notice, consent for sharing, cross-border transfer notices |
| **Global (app stores)** | Apple App Store and Google Play policies | Account deletion in the app, privacy labels that match the app, in-app purchase rules (see `mobile.md`) |

## Report it this way
- Put the code-detectable traps (fonts, trackers before consent, embeds, overlays, missing cancel link, missing Impressum) under 🔴 or 🟡 like any other finding, each with a fix prompt. Most are **safe to auto-fix** (self-hosting fonts, gating scripts behind consent), except changes users will see.
- Put the region rows that apply under **Needs a lawyer**, phrased as questions: *"We have users in Quebec: do our privacy notice and checkout need French versions?"*
